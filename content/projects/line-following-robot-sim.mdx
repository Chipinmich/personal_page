## The Goal

Simulate a differential-drive robot following a painted line, and compare a
hand-tuned controller against a learned one on the same track.

## Why This Project

Simulation removes the parts of robotics that make iteration slow — batteries,
wiring, physical damage — while keeping the parts that make it hard: noisy
sensors, delayed actuation, and the gap between what the model assumes and what
the world does.

## Planned Structure

- **Physics.** A minimal differential-drive model with configurable wheel slip.
- **Sensing.** A small array of downward-facing brightness sensors with noise.
- **Baseline.** A PID controller tuned by hand, used as the bar to beat.
- **Learned controller.** The same task, with the control policy learned instead
  of tuned.
- **Comparison.** Both controllers, identical tracks, repeated runs.
